WPL LORE

It began with a scooter party. A tribal era of Hide and Seek and Apocalypse that birthed a ten-year legacy.

We weathered the Waterview War. We survived the Dark Ages of the pandemic. Through blood, rock-throwing, and the eventual rise of Football, the Unification was achieved.

But peace is a fragile thing. This is the history of the Waterview Premier League.
