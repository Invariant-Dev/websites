The Eras of Lore

Feb 2017

The Scooter Party Period

The dawn of lore. The Tribal Period consisting of Hide And Seek, Tag, and Apocalypse.

Jan 2018 - Feb 2019

Increasing Infrastructure

The introduction of Virus, Floor is Lava, and Cops and Robbers. A unification of members still present today.

Feb 2019 - July 2019

The Waterview War

The group splits. Cockfighting, rock throwing, and sticks as weapons. Peace achieved only by interference from Aakash and Xiao An.

April 2020 - June 2022

The Dark Ages

Interaction prohibited. Legends depart. The true test of Waterview. Only the real OGs survived.

June 2022 - Dec 2023

Cleanup & The WPL

The actual term WPL is coined. The era of Football begins. Dhyanam refuses to join.

Jan 2024 - June 2025

Peace, at last...

Dhyanam yields. Nam learns football. A fragile, different peace settles over the land.

The Fall of Kickgyan

June 2025: A major argument with Vihaan echoes through the grounds. Judgment must be passed.
