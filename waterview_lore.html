WPL CHRONICLES

It is a period of great shifting in Waterview. From the tribal beginnings of Hide and Seek, to the dark days of rock-throwing warfare, and the great silence of the Quarantine...

Legends have fallen. New blood has risen. The Unification is complete, but the peace is fragile. The departure of Kickgyan echoes through the realm.

The history of the Waterview Premier League is recorded below. A ten-year journey of friendship, betrayal, and football.
